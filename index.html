About the model

A zonal energy-balance model over 18 equal-area bands. Longwave radiation uses a semi-grey two-stream fit anchored to modern Earth (240 W/m²), Venus (161 W/m²) and the Simpson–Nakajima runaway limit of 283 W/m² — which the fit reproduces rather than imposes, so the runaway greenhouse emerges from the physics.

Slow processes run on the same accelerated clock: the carbonate–silicate thermostat (~1 Myr), snowball CO₂ build-up (5–50 Myr), and hydrogen escape (10⁸–10⁹ yr to lose an ocean). Physics advances on simulated time only — the trajectory is identical at any frame rate.
