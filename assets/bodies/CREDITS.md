# Altdev body-map credits

## Moon

`moon.jpg` is the 2048 × 1024 colour map from NASA Goddard Scientific
Visualization Studio's [CGI Moon Kit](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/), downloaded
from the kit's [`lroc_color_2k.jpg`](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/lroc_color_2k.jpg).
It is a natural-colour, Hapke-normalized mosaic made from Lunar Reconnaissance
Orbiter Camera Wide Angle Camera data, adjusted by the SVS team toward the Moon's
appearance to human vision and centred at 0° longitude for texture mapping.

NASA SVS credits: Ernie Wright (USRA, visualizer) and Noah Petro (NASA/GSFC,
scientist); source data from the LRO/LROC science team. NASA content is used under
the [NASA media usage guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/).
